Evoking magic is quick and dangerous, both to adversaries and to the caster themselves. Evoking requires the use of Mana and the know-how to channel the forces of magic through your will and being with sudden intensity and unadulterated intention.

## Mana Pool
Your Mana Pool represents your capacity to channel magic safely. You Magical Methodology determines how great your Mana Pool may be and how this resource is regenerated. Even when your Mana Pool is at zero you may still cast spells but must roll for Mana Feedback for each unpaid mana.

## Mana Limit
Your Mana Limit determines the greatest amount of mana you can channel within a single round, whether you cast multiple spells or only one. When casting a spell would push you beyond your Mana Limit that spell roll is replaced with a Fumble and you must roll for Mana Feedback. Your Magical Methodology determines how you calculate your Mana Limit.

## Mana Feedback
Whenever you suffer Mana Feedback, roll 1d6 with no modifiers and apply the result.

| Mana Feedback Roll | Result.<br>                                                                                                                   |
| -----------------: | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|              **6** | Re-roll. If you roll **3**, **4**, or **5**, double the effects, but only once regardless of how many re-rolls are performed. |
|              **5** | Suffer the effects of rolling both **3** and **4**.                                                                           |
|              **4** | Take **3** points of unblockable damage.                                                                                      |
|              **3** | Mark the **Tired** Ailing Survival Condition.                                                                                 |
|              **2** | Nothing.                                                                                                                      |
|              **1** | Nothing.                                                                                                                      |

If you would suffer Mana Feedback multiple times at once, simply make this roll multiple times, resolving each separately but immediately.
## Casting Spells
Each spell is a specific magical effect with its own mana cost, associated magical domains, and unique effects. To cast a spell, pay its mana cost and make a Spellcasting Check. Your Magical Methodology prescribes which Attribute you use to cast your spells.

On a Hit, the spell succeeds and resolves its effects as described. On a Miss the Mana is spent but the spell does not take effect.
## Empowering Spells
Most spells have optional upgrades listed with it called Empowerments - effectively making each spell its own modifiable toolkit. 

As you cast a spell you can choose to upgrade it by paying the additional mana cost of one or more Empowerments. Each Empowerment also raises the difficulty of your Spellcasting Check, but does not count as a Hindrance - it simply is a harder spell to cast.

If your spell succeeds, the spell resolves normally but with additional or modified effects as described in the Empowerment. If an Empowered spell fails, suffer Mana Feedback once.

## Magical Methodologies

###### Innate Spell
Your magic is not based on training, practice, or study. Magic is part of your blood. This might occur as the result of magical experimentation, exposure to raw primordial energies, or bet he result of bio-magical physiology.

You know a single spell chosen when you gain this trait. You may cast this spell without paying its mana cost. Your Mana Limit for this spell is equal to your Wound Threshold. The Attribute you use for Spellcasting Checks when casting this spell is Spirit.

###### Awakened
Your magic is born from deep attunement to the infinite possibilities found within your mind, soul, and the world around you. Whether through qi, psionics, or supernatural harmony with the primordial forces of nature, you shape reality by entering states of profound resonance rather than through study or instinct alone.

Your Mana Pool represents your resonance - the harmony you have cultivated with these greater forces. You have a Mana Pool equal to {Potency + Spirit + Intuition * 2}. Your Mana Limit is Spirit + Intuition. You regain 1d6 Mana +1 for each unmarked Ailing Condition every time you roll Daily Recovery. Your Spellcasting Attribute is Spirit.

Awakened mages possess Mantles: distinct states of body, mind, and spirit that bring them into accord with particular supernatural principles. While a Mantle is active, you gain its passive benefit and may cast its associated spells by spending Mana. You may have only one Mantle active at a time.

When you first gain this trait you know 1 Mantle. You can take this trait multiple to know an additional Mantle each time.

You can cast ritual magic.

###### Spell Wright
Your magic is diligently earned through consistent practice, study, experimentation, and refinement. You draw on the ambient energies of the world around you to summon up Mana for your spells and utilize contraptions and enchantments to directly support your spell casting. The Attribute you use for Spellcasting Checks is Reason.

Your Mana Pool is 0, and never gains mana on its own. Instead you Gather Mana as an action.

<u>**Gather Mana**</u>: make a spellcasting check and divide the total by 4, ignoring any remainder. Add the result to your Mana Pool. Gathering Mana requires a full turn. This mana is visible as swirling energy around you and dissipates after a few minutes if unused.

You Mana Limit is equal to Potency + Reason.

You can safely hold only so much mana. If your Mana Pool ever exceeds twice your Mana Limit, immediately interrupt the current action. You lose all gathered mana and make a roll for Mana Feedback. This is called Mana Overflow.

When you first gain this trait you know 1 spell. If you take this trait during character creation, you start play with an additional 2 spells for each magical Background.

You can learn additional spells through play.

You can cast ritual magic.

###### Pact Magician
Your magic is the direct result of association with, and likely favor from, a magical arch-being bestowing you with power. Each pact is a different trait, and may have different rules for how you access magic. Some may even change your spell abilities from time to time.
