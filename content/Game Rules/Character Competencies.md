## Attributes
You Attributes help set the tone for how you function best in the world of Estaron. One of your Attributes is selected to be a bonus to every Check you make. That bonus is equal to the Attributes Rank. Its Rank is determined as part of the character creation process and it may change as your character grows.

There are eight Attributes, each suggests it's natural types of applications:

- **Power** represents strength, force, and explosiveness. 
- **Agility** is about coordination, precision, and deftness. 
- **Vitality** includes health, endurance and constitution. 
- **Potency** affects the efficacy and power of spells.
- **Reason** suggests logic, procedure, & knowledge. Tied to strategic searching.
- **Intuition** implies instinct, tacit understanding, and creativity. Tied to passive perception.
- **Presence** is tied to social atmosphere, projecting intention or hiding it, entertaining, & leading.
- **Spirit** is largely about your focus, faith, willpower, and mettle.

Often, depending on how you address a problem, multiple different Attributes could be used. The Attribute that most directly applies in comparison to the other Attributes is what you use when you roll the dice - if two Attributes are truly equal, use the greater one.

Each Attribute will have a Rank between 0 and 5. Anything 2 or better is at least noteworthy. Anything 4 and above is exceptional. 

## Backgrounds
Your origin is described with an Archetype and a Connection. An Archetype was your job or role - it's how you lived. A Connection can be an organization or group, a location, or a major event.

When a Background is created it can look like:

- Refugee of the Hylandian Wars
- Pirate of the Azure Seas
- Horizon Chasers Guild, Wilderness Guide
- Arkel-Leuom Street Sweeper
- Demon Hunter from The Shattered Isles
- Blackwater League Merchant

If either your Archetype or Connection would suggest experience that is helpful to a Check you are making then you do not suffer the Untrained penalty.

You Background should be considered as useful for skills, knowledge, culture, and perception in related areas.

## Specialties
A Specialty is a specific skill such as swordplay with which you have a knack for. Whenever you make a Check centered on your Specialty you may re-roll one of the die used for the roll and take the better result. This applies passively, every time.

While you have Advantage, handle Advantage first. Then apply your Specialty to see if it gives you a greater final result or not.
## Talents
Talents also are a bonus for specific skill - in fact you can have both a Talent and a Specialty in the same skill. The difference is that a Talent adds its Rank to a roll much like Attributes do, except only when its skill is clearly relevant. 

Both Specialties & Talents are Traits you can purchase for your character, or gain as you grow.
