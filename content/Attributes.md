You Attributes help set the tone for how you function best in the world of Estaron. One of your Attributes is selected to be a bonus to every Check you make. That bonus is equal to the Attributes Rank. Its Rank is determined as part of the character creation process and it may change as your character grows.

There are eight Attributes, each suggests it's natural types of applications:

- **Power** represents strength, force, and explosiveness. 
- **Agility** is about coordination, precision, and deftness. 
- **Vitality** includes health, endurance and constitution. 
- **Potency** affects the efficacy and power of spells.
- **Reason** suggests logic, procedure, & knowledge. Tied to strategic searching.
- **Intuition** implies instinct, tacit understanding, and creativity. Tied to passive perception.
- **Presence** is tied to social atmosphere, projecting intention or hiding it, entertaining, & leading.
- **Spirit** is largely about your focus, faith, willpower, and mettle.

These Attributes set the tone for ***how*** you are most likely to succeed - both where you are skilled and where you are outside of your comfort zone. They also affect some other numbers on your character sheet. Your other qualities start to fill in ***what*** you are actually good at in more detail.

Often, depending on how you address a problem, multiple different Attributes could be used. The Attribute that most directly applies in comparison to the other Attributes is what you use when you roll the dice - if two Attributes are truly equal, use the greater one.

Each Attribute will have a Rank between 0 and 5. Anything 2 or better is at least noteworthy. Anything 4 and above is exceptional.
