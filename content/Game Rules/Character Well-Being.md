## Health Track
The limits of your survival capability are measured overall with a Health Track. You have 5 levels of health, each represented by a heart in your Health Track. When you lose a health level, cross out one of your hearts.

While you have two or less hearts remaining in your Health Track you are Worn. Characters who are Worn suffer Disadvantage on all Attribute Checks.

You will most often lose health levels by taking damage or from the accumulation of stress, fatigue, and hunger.

Other things such exposure to the elements and sickness may also feed into your degrading health.
### Wound Threshold
After an attack hits you, your attacker will deal damage. Your armor, if you have any, affords at least some level of Protection. Damage that makes it through counts towards your Wound Threshold.

When you take damage, track it on your character sheet. Your Wound Threshold equals **2 + Vitality + (½ Spirit)**.

Whenever accumulated damage equals or exceeds your threshold, lose one health level then subtract your Wound Threshold from the damage total that you're tracking. If sufficient damage remains you may have to repeat this process multiple times.

For example, if you have a threshold of 3 and take 5 damage, you’ll lose a health level and tally the other 2 points of damage. If you previously had taken 1 damage, instead you’ll end up losing 2 health levels with no tally’s for damage remaining.
### Ailing Conditions
On your character sheet, underneath your health level hearts, are 3 conditions which each have two checkboxes besides them. These are known as Ailing Conditions.

Ailing Conditions have no penalty of their own. Instead they are a buffer measuring what your body and mind can endure. They include Tired, Hungry, and Stressed. 

When you gain an Ailing Condition mark one of its checkboxes, if both are already marked instead gain one of the other Ailing Conditions of your choice - if all are fully marked lose a health level.

When you recover an Ailing Condition, erase one of the marks in its checkboxes.

**Ailing Condition Triggers**
 - You mark Tired if you didn’t get at least poor sleep (~ more than 4 hours) or you Fumble a check for Endurance. 
- You mark Hungry if you either didn’t hydrate or get at least 2 poor meals (rations) during the day.
- You mark Distressed when you fail a check for morale such as due to violence, horror, despair, or deep disgust.

**Ailing Condition Recovery**
- Recover Tired if you had plenty of sleep (~9+ hours) in Comfortable conditions or took a day off from adventure somewhere you can let your guard down.
- Recover Hungry if you had 3 or more basic meals, or feasted on an especially filling and hearty meal.
- Recover Stressed if you re-centered yourself on your goals or faith, worked and prepared for the challenges ahead, or you spent time playing, supporting, and bonding with your fellows.

**Flourishing Assets**
For each Ailing Condition with no checkboxes marked you gain a +1 Asset. These Flourishing Assets are a bonus to certain checks for you and medical care you receive.
## Daily Recovery
Each dawn you make a Vitality Check for Daily Recovery with a difficulty of 13. 

- On a Hit, restore one lost Health Level. 
- On a Triumph restore 2. 

In certain areas of the world the difficulty of this check may be altered or certain environments might be considered an Asset or Hindrance to this roll.

If dawn occurs during an encounter, the GM will have you make this check either right before or right after.
## Medical Aid
A caretaker or physician may make checks, with time and necessary resources, to tend to the needs of the suffering. 

When a caretaker has basic medial supplies or can improvise them and gives medical aid to another character, the patient clears any leftover tallied damage that did not meet the Wound Threshold (and therefore, did not become a lost health level).

While a caretaker may not directly restore one of your lost health levels they can provide  monitoring and care that improves your chances of recovery.

When a caretaker provides care specific to their patients necessities by explicitly treating the ailment, cleaning wounds, and re-dresses wounds or re-applies treatment as needed over the course of a day or more they may make a Medicine Check.

- On a Hit, the patient gains a +2 Asset on their next checks for each of Daily Recovery, Sickness, and Death. 
- On a Triumph they gain Advantage in addition to the +2.

 The base difficulty for such Checks is at least 13. Special effects & circumstances may modify the difficulty of checks for healing like normal.

Tools and time are almost always required, although some resources may be improvised. Plus, filth and infection love to get in the way when they can.
## Sickness
When a character passes through infected areas or is subjected directly to filthy environments and conditions they may contract an illness. They may also develop an illness as a cause of natural living - whether from allergies and common colds or unknown sources.

Whenever a character experiences situations that are likely to make them sick, they must make a Sickness Check. Sickness Checks are always made immediately after the next Morning Recovery.

- On a Miss they contract an appropriate illness or one they already have worsens.
- On a Hit they do not get sick, or they progress removing an already existing sickness.

Your character sheet has a Sickness Track that allows for 3 stages of sickness. When a character first contracts an illness they mark the Stage 1 of their Sickness Track on their character sheet and mark Tired. Each time the illness worsens they move the track up to the next stage and mark Tired again.

While your Sickness Track is at Stage 2 you suffer a 1 point Hindrance on all Attribute Checks. This penalty also applies against Medicine Checks made to aid you.

While your Sickness Track is at Stage 3 your Daily Recovery Check has modified results as your body is required to dedicate its efforts more fully towards defeating the illness. Additionally, if a Stage 3 Sickness would worsen, instead lose a health level.

When you make your Daily Recovery Check, the first time you would recover a lost health level instead move your Sickness Track back a stage once. After Daily Recovery, still make your Sickness Check like normal.
## Elemental Exposure

Exposure Track where there is minimal, moderate, or severe exposure that can then be tied into common and rare magical weather effects  alike.*

A character is Comfortable when they are sheltered from the elements, free from immediate danger, and have a cozy place to rest for several hours.

A Comfortable character gains Advantage on Daily Recovery, Sickness, & Death Checks or recover Tired more easily.

A character is Exposed when they are insufficiently protected from severe weather or prolonged adverse conditions. While Exposed you suffer Disadvantage on Vitality Checks. 
## Stabilizing & Death
If you lose all 5 Health Levels you are Incapacitated and Dying. 

At the end of the scene you will make a Death Check - the base difficulty is 13, and you’ll roll the greater between Spirit or Vitality.

- On a Fumble, lose your 6th health level. 
- On a Miss, you’re still unstable. The next time you would roll for Daily Recovery instead make a new Death Check. 
- On a Hit, you stabilize. You're still incapacitated & don’t regain any lost health levels yet, but this trial won’t be the one to lay you low. 
- On a Triumph, benefit from the effects of a Hit plus you gain Advantage on your next Daily Recovery Check.

If you lose a 6th health level, you die. Your soul will visit the Hall of Journeys, you will have clarity about your life and potential, and there you may return home as you pass through the doors of eternity. 

If you did not die but also did not stabilize, Medical Aid can help with future Death Checks like normal.
