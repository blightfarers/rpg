Your origin is described with an Archetype and a Connection. An Archetype was your job or role - it's how you lived. A Connection can be an organization or group, a location, or a major event.

When a Background is created it can look like:

- Refugee of the Hylandian Wars
- Pirate of the Azure Seas
- Horizon Chasers Guild, Wilderness Guide
- Arkel-Leuom Street Sweeper
- Demon Hunter from The Shattered Isles
- Blackwater League Merchant

If either your Archetype or Connection would suggest experience that is helpful to a Check you are making then you do not suffer the Untrained penalty.

You Background should be considered as useful for skills, knowledge, culture, and perception in related areas.
