Each day is separated into 4 daily stages with 2 phases each - an early phase and a late phase. The stages are Morning, Day, Evening, and Night. 

Each daily stage continually and gradually flows into the next and represents roughly the same amount of time as another - somewhere approximating 6 hours. The early and late phases split this in half.

When something in the game references Dawn it means the moment where Late Night ends and Early Morning begins, while Dusk is the moment Late Day ends and Early Evening begins.

At Dawn the sun begins to rise in the east, and at Dusk it begins to set in the west.

As you take action time moves on. The Facilitator will periodically describe the skies, the feeling of the passage of time, and apparent changes in weather where appropriate. 

When you cannot clearly view the skies they might purposefully leave the sense of the passage of time ambiguous after a certain point.

When you are not in a moment-to-moment scene actions your group commits to might be performed across the span of a sub-phase, daily stage, or more. Moments such as shopping, travel, research, crafting, or exploring filler are good examples. The Facilitator will summarize what was experienced, gained or lost, or whatever else changed before transitioning to the next moment of opportunity.

Time is explicitly built into the game as a narrative cue for health, weather, navigation, factions and other behind-the-scene world actions, and to create contrast opportunities. It is intended to become a natural part of a healthy game flow between tension and challenge, rest and camaraderie, discovery and exploration, and so forth.

In particular, your Facilitator may make Facilitator Checks for weather, faction actions, and how much your attracting additional encounters as appropriate for the adventuring area.

Facilitators - weather is an important consideration both for setting the tone of a scene and for adding obstacles or modifying relative difficulty in an encounter. That means it's better to be purposeful with how you employ weather. 

That said, [[empire-weather.pdf |Empire Weather]] is a simple tool for generating weather randomly. It can be useful to roll for weather in advance so that you can telegraph it throughout the stages and phases of the day, or even the day before - that said, for sudden storms rolling for the current phase or stage can be okay, just understand that its natures equivalent of a sneak attack to your players.
